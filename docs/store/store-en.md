# Cloudsea: Spirit Gathering · Steam Store Page (English)

## Short description (≤ 300 characters)
The Old Land sank beneath the Cloudsea and the Blight swallowed the spirits. As a new Islekeeper, farm and build on your floating isle, befriend neighboring Islekeepers, raise one-of-a-kind spirits, and rekindle five lighthouses in turn-based battles. Up to 4 players co-op.

## About This Game
After the Old Land sank, people made their homes on floating isles above the Cloudsea, living alongside small creatures called spirits. Then the lighthouses went dark, the Blight crept in, and the spirits it touched lost themselves.

You are a newly arrived Islekeeper, starting on a tiny isle with nothing but its Island Core:
- **Farm & build** — grow crops, craft goods, expand your Home Isle, and let your spirits lend a hand in fields and workshops.
- **Collect & raise** — 40 spirit forms, each individual with its own parts, talents and nature. Breeding passes traits on (with the odd mutation), and spirits of resonating elements can even cross-breed.
- **Turn-based battles** — five weather elements counter and empower each other, and controlling the field weather decides the fight. Cloudlords shift phases and act twice per turn.
- **Five regions, one story** — Sun Plains, Wind Valley, Rain Marsh, Thunder Cliffs and the Mist Sea. Beat each Lighthouse Warden, purify the Cloudlord, rekindle the lighthouse, and watch the fog lift to reveal the next region on your Star Chart.
- **Neighbors with lives of their own** — 24 Islekeepers with distinct personalities and stories; 6–10 of them settle into each world. They tend their own isles, drop by your home, post orders and challenge you to battles.
- **Co-op for up to 4** — the host's world is everyone's world. Raid Cloudlords together and team up in wild battles.
- **Serverless sharing** — turn a spirit, a team or your whole isle into a short code. Visit a friend's isle, or challenge their team.

## Features
- Every visual is generated by code: low-poly isles, spirits and characters are procedurally modeled and rigged.
- Fully playable offline. Optional "Spirit Mode" lets Islekeepers think and talk in real time using your own AI service.
- Controller support, English and Simplified Chinese, Steam Achievements and Steam Cloud.

## Early Access
**Why Early Access?** The core loop, the five-region story and co-op are complete and playable. We want to tune pacing, balance and game feel with players before adding more content.

**Approximately how long will this game be in Early Access?** About 6–12 months.

**How is the full version planned to differ?** Planned additions: music and sound, drifting isles and market isles, more Islekeeper stories and dialogue, friendly PvP and trading, more spirit forms and Cloudlords, and controller support in build mode.

**What is the current state of the Early Access version?** You can play from a new game all the way to the finale where all five lighthouses are rekindled (roughly 8–15 hours depending on play style), solo or with up to 4 players. There is currently **no music or sound**.

**Will the game be priced differently during and after Early Access?** The price may rise slightly at full release; Early Access buyers will not pay again.

**How are you planning on involving the Community?** Through the Steam discussions and regular patch notes; balance changes are guided by player feedback and automated battle statistics.

## AI Generated Content Disclosure
**Pre-generated content**
- Islekeeper personas, story scenes and the dialogue library (about 1,750 Chinese lines) as well as all English translations were drafted with AI assistance during development, then reviewed by the developer against the design guidelines and validated (every line passes content-rule and length checks) before shipping.
- The game's art (models, animation, UI icons) is **not** AI-generated: it is built procedurally by code.

**Live-generated content**
- The optional "Spirit Mode" is off by default. When enabled, the game uses an AI service **configured by the player** (OpenAI Chat Completions compatible) to generate Islekeeper dialogue, daily plans and memory summaries in real time.
- Guardrails: a system prompt constrains character and topics; output must be structured JSON that the game rules validate one by one (Islekeepers may only choose from allowed actions and can never change game numbers); per-line length limits; a sensitive-word and out-of-character filter (applied by the host when generating and by each guest when displaying); any failure falls back to the pre-written dialogue library.
- Generated lines are labeled "AI generated" in the dialogue box, and players can block any line with one click.
- The player's API key is stored only on their own computer (OS-level encryption), is sent only to the address the player entered, and never goes into save files or to other players in co-op.
